# Business Requirements

## Project Objective

Design and develop an interactive Power BI business intelligence solution that provides RetailMart Australia's executives and operational managers with a centralised view of sales, profitability, targets, stores, products, customers and returns.

The solution should support both high-level executive monitoring and detailed investigation of the factors driving business performance.

## Primary Stakeholders

### Executive Management
- Monitor revenue, profit and profit margin.
- Assess year-on-year business performance.
- Track performance against revenue, order and margin targets.
- Identify significant areas of business underperformance.

### Sales & Regional Management
- Compare performance across stores and regions.
- Identify the strongest-performing stores.
- Investigate sales and order trends.
- Analyse category and subcategory performance.

### Commercial & Product Management
- Identify high-revenue and high-margin products.
- Identify products generating strong revenue but weak margins.
- Compare profitability across product categories.
- Investigate product performance at different levels of the product hierarchy.

### Customer & Operations Management
- Monitor purchasing customer volumes and customer value.
- Compare customer behaviour across loyalty tiers.
- Monitor return volumes and return rates.
- Identify the largest causes of refund expenditure.

## Core Business Questions

The report was designed to answer the following questions:

1. How are revenue and profit performing over time?
2. How does current performance compare with previous periods?
3. Is the business achieving its revenue, order and profit-margin targets?
4. Which stores generate the strongest revenue performance?
5. Which product categories and individual products drive revenue and profitability?
6. Are any high-revenue products operating at comparatively weak margins?
7. How do sales and order volumes change throughout the year?
8. Which customer loyalty segments generate the greatest value?
9. How are customer volumes changing over time?
10. How significant are product returns relative to sales activity?
11. What are the major causes and financial impacts of returns?

## Functional Requirements

The Power BI solution should:

- provide an executive-level overview of key business KPIs
- support year-on-year and comparison-period analysis
- compare actual performance against business targets
- allow users to filter analysis by year, region and product category
- provide dynamic store rankings
- support product-level profitability analysis
- allow drill-down from product category to subcategory
- provide customer segmentation by loyalty tier
- analyse returns according to return date
- support cross-filtering and interactive exploration between visuals
- provide additional contextual information through tooltips

## Report Structure

The final report contains three analytical pages:

1. **Executive Overview** — high-level business performance and target monitoring.
2. **Store & Product Analysis** — detailed store, product, category and sales-trend analysis.
3. **Customer & Returns Analysis** — customer-value segmentation and returns analysis.
