# Business Requirements

## Project Objective

Design and develop an interactive Power BI solution that gives RetailMart Australia's executives and operational managers a centralised view of sales, profitability, customers, products, stores, targets, promotions and returns.

## Primary Stakeholders

### Chief Executive Officer
- Monitor revenue, profit, margin and year-on-year growth.
- Identify material risks and underperforming regions.
- Track performance against targets.

### Sales Director
- Compare stores, regions, products and sales channels.
- Identify high-revenue but low-margin products.
- Assess discount and promotion effectiveness.

### Marketing Manager
- Understand customer segments and loyalty behaviour.
- Compare new and repeat customer performance.
- Identify commercially valuable customer cohorts.

### Regional Managers
- Monitor store performance against targets.
- Investigate returns, product mix and staff-assisted sales.
- Identify stores requiring management attention.

## Core Questions

1. Is revenue and profit growing?
2. Which regions and stores are missing targets?
3. Which categories and products drive revenue and margin?
4. How do promotions and discounts affect profitability?
5. Which customer segments generate the most value?
6. Which channels have the highest sales and return rates?
7. What are the main causes and financial impact of returns?
